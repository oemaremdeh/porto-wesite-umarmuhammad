My Story

Passionate About Crafting the Web

Hi! I'm Alex Morgan, a Full Stack Developer and UI/UX Designer based in Jakarta, Indonesia. I have over 5 years of experience building digital products that are both functional and visually stunning.

My journey started when I built my first website at age 15. Since then, I've worked with startups, agencies, and established companies to deliver high-quality web solutions. I believe that great software is not just about code — it's about empathy for the user and attention to detail.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me hiking, reading about design psychology, or experimenting with new technologies. I'm always excited to take on new challenges and collaborate with inspiring people.
